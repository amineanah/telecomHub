💡 RAN (Radio Access Network) Technology

Comprehensive guide to Radio Access Network architecture, components, and technologies that connect mobile devices to the core network.

RAN Architecture

Radio Access Network consists of base stations (BS), antennas, and transmission infrastructure connecting mobile users to the operator's core network.

  • Traditional RAN: eNodeB (4G), gNodeB (5G)
  • C-RAN: Centralized, Cloud-based architecture
  • Distributed RAN: Split processing (CU/DU/RU)
  • Coverage: Macro, micro, small cells
  • Antennas: MIMO arrays, beamforming capabilities
  • Channel Bandwidth: 5MHz - 100MHz+ (5G)

Massive MIMO

Advanced antenna technology using 64, 128, or 256+ antenna elements. Dramatically increases capacity, coverage, and energy efficiency.

  • Antenna Count: 64x64, 128x128, 256x256
  • Technology: Planar array antennas
  • Beamforming: Precise beam steering and tracking
  • Capacity Gain: 5-10x improvement
  • Coverage: Extended edge coverage
  • Latency: Reduced through efficient processing

5G NR RAN

5G New Radio standard delivering enhanced spectral efficiency, ultra-low latency, and massive connectivity for diverse use cases.

  • Sub-6 GHz: 450 MHz - 6 GHz bands
  • mmWave: 24-100 GHz spectrum
  • Numerology: Scalable 15/30/60/120 kHz
  • Modulation: QPSK to 1024QAM
  • Duplex: FDD/TDD, Full-duplex capable
  • Slicing: eMBB, URLLC, mMTC support

🚀 RAN Evolution & Innovations

Open RAN (O-RAN)

Open and disaggregated RAN architecture enabling multi-vendor ecosystems. RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC) provides AI/ML-based optimization. Breaks vendor lock-in and accelerates innovation in RAN deployments.

Small Cell Technology

Femtocells, picocells, and microcells complement macrocells for dense urban coverage and capacity enhancement. Smaller footprints, lower power consumption, easier deployment in challenging environments.

Handover & Mobility

Seamless user mobility across cells, layers, and technologies. Intra-band/inter-band handover, dual connectivity, and network slicing ensure quality of experience. Critical for 5G services.

RAN Performance Monitoring

Real-time KPI tracking: SINR, Coverage, Capacity, Interference, Latency, Handover Success Rate. Drive testing, OMC monitoring, and RAN analytics ensure optimal network performance and customer satisfaction.
