Engineering references & further reading

Use the official publications as the authority for specifications. This page provides learning summaries and calculators, not vendor-specific configuration instructions.

  • For real propagation planning, use an appropriate terrain/clutter model (for example, a validated operator model); FSPL is a simplified baseline.
  • RSRP, RSRQ and SINR interpretation depends on measurement method, radio configuration, load and vendor counters.
  • Validate every design against current licensed spectrum, equipment limits, local regulation and the applicable 3GPP release.
