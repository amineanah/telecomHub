AAU / RRU MODULE

🟣 Active Antenna Unit (AAU 64T64R)

Integrates base station radio transceivers and active antenna array into a single enclosure. Supports Massive MIMO beamforming, digital phase control, and multi-band 5G NR transmission.

OPERATING BAND n78 (3.5 GHz) / n41
CONFIG / CHANNELS 64T64R Massive MIMO
INTERFACE eCPRI 25 Gbps Fiber
POWER SUPPLY -48V DC Nominal

📋 Quality Assurance Inspection Checkpoints

  • Verify mechanical tilt, azimuth angle, and structural mounting torque.
  • Inspect eCPRI optical fiber connection and waterproof boot sealing.
  • Check DC power feeder polarity and grounding lug connection to tower bar.
  • Ensure clearance for thermal dissipation and wind-load stability.
💡 Field Audit Tip: Always cross-check installed serial numbers against ISDP deployment acceptance records[cite: 1].
