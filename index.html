PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Amine Janah

Telecom Engineer | Quality Assurance | 5G & RAN

Telecom professional with more than 3 years of experience in Huawei, specializing in telecom hardware installation assurance, site quality control, 5G deployment, RAN technologies and field operations.

Experienced with 2G/3G/4G/5G environments, Massive MIMO, RF optimization, site modernization, subcontractor coordination, EHS requirements and telecom project follow-up. • Lead and coordinate quality control activities for telecom network deployment and hardware installation.
• Conduct field inspections and site visits to ensure compliance with technical specifications, installation standards, and EHS requirements.
• Monitor telecom installation activities from MOS to PAC, ensuring quality throughout the deployment lifecycle.
• Manage and coordinate SBC/subcontractor teams, providing technical guidance and following up on installation progress.
• Control installation quality for 4G and 5G RAN equipment, including FDD, TDD, Massive MIMO and modernization activities.
• Follow the technical implementation of solutions including FDD 2600, TDD 8T8R and Massive MIMO 64T64R.
• Perform quality inspections for Huawei telecom equipment, including RRU/RFU, BBU, AAU and associated site infrastructure.
• Follow site modernization and capacity-upgrade activities across different LTE frequency bands.
• Ensure proper implementation of PPE, EHS procedures, site safety requirements and installation standards.
• Monitor material availability and coordinate with relevant teams regarding CIAG and supply-chain activities.
• Follow technical site studies, implementation, integration, quality control and final acceptance.
• Use U2020 and ISDP platforms for deployment monitoring, follow-up and operational activities.
• Prepare and follow up on quality findings, corrective actions, site observations and acceptance requirements.
• Support continuous improvement of installation quality and field execution standards.
