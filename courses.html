How to use these telecom courses

Start with the fundamentals, read each lesson, then use the knowledge checks to test your understanding. Course progress is saved locally in your browser, so use the same device if you want to resume later. These lessons are educational practice materials, not vendor accreditation or proof of professional competence.

Suggested learning path

  1. Mobile networks: identify the main functions of the radio access network and how 4G LTE differs from 5G NR.
  2. RF planning: review coverage, interference, antenna configuration, and why field measurements matter.
  3. Fiber and transport: learn the role of optical links, connectors, and loss budgets in telecom infrastructure.
  4. Site quality and safety: practice inspection principles, documentation, and escalation of hazards. Always follow site-specific procedures and qualified supervision.

After completing a lesson

Write down one concept you can explain without notes, one question to investigate, and one practical example from a network or site. Use the technical articles to explore topics in greater depth and the practice exams for additional revision.
