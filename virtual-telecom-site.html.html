Active Antenna Units (AAU)

Combines passive antenna arrays with active RF transceivers. Utilizes 64T64R Massive MIMO technology to form targeted beamforming patterns toward active user equipment.

🔵

eCPRI Front-Haul Fiber

High-speed optical interconnect linking the top-tower AAU with the ground Baseband Unit. Runs eCPRI protocols carrying digitized IQ baseband data at 10Gbps/25Gbps.

⚡

DC Power Architecture

Industrial rectifiers convert incoming 220V/380V AC utility power to -48V DC. Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery banks provide emergency site backup.
