⚡ Power & Energy Technology

Comprehensive guide to power systems, energy efficiency, and sustainability in telecom networks. Critical for site operations and operational costs.

Power Supply Systems

Reliable power delivery infrastructure for telecom sites with multiple redundancy levels ensuring continuous network operation.

  • AC Power: 230V/400V grid supply
  • DC Power: -48V/+24V distribution systems
  • UPS Systems: Battery backup for continuity
  • Rectifiers: AC to DC conversion 95%+ efficiency
  • Redundancy: N+1 or N+2 configurations
  • Monitoring: Real-time power parameters tracking

Battery Technology

Battery systems providing backup power during grid failures. Critical for site availability and emergency operations.

  • Lead-Acid Batteries: Traditional, cost-effective
  • Lithium-Ion: Higher energy density, longer lifespan
  • Capacity: 2-6 hours backup typical (site dependent)
  • Charging Systems: Intelligent charge controllers
  • Maintenance: Regular testing, equalization cycles
  • Temperature Control: Optimal performance range

Energy Efficiency

Optimization strategies reducing power consumption and operational expenses while maintaining network performance.

  • Sleep Modes: Base station power reduction off-peak
  • Efficient Amplifiers: GaN, LDMOS technologies
  • Cooling Optimization: Free-air cooling, economizers
  • LED Lighting: Reduced site lighting power
  • Smart Power Distribution: Per-equipment control
  • Renewable Energy: Solar, wind integration

🚀 Advanced Power Management

Energy Management Systems (EMS)

Centralized monitoring and control of site power consumption. Real-time dashboards showing power usage, battery status, and efficiency metrics. Enables predictive maintenance and optimization strategies.

Renewable Energy Integration

Solar panels and wind turbines reduce grid dependency and operational costs. Hybrid systems combine renewable sources with grid power and batteries. Critical for remote sites and sustainability goals.

Power Quality Management

Protection against voltage fluctuations, harmonics, and transients. UPS systems provide voltage regulation and filtering. Critical for equipment protection and network reliability.

Site Power Audit

Comprehensive analysis of power consumption patterns identifying optimization opportunities. Measures PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) and identifies efficiency improvements for cost reduction.
