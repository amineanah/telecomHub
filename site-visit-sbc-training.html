📌 Key Moments from the Visit

🦺 Safety First - PPE & Procedures

Before any hands-on work began, we conducted a comprehensive safety briefing covering all critical aspects of working on an active telecom site:

  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements - helmets, high-visibility vests, safety shoes
  • Safe work distances around live RF and electrical equipment
  • Exclusion zone protocols and awareness procedures
  • Tool safety checks and inspection procedures
  • Lockout/permit procedures for energized equipment work

All participants wore proper PPE and followed established lockout/permit-to-work procedures throughout the training session. This set a strong example of safety culture in field operations.

Team briefing session before the hands-on SBC training activities

🔌 Equipment Walkthrough & Cable Management

The trainer provided an in-depth explanation of SBC internals and subsystems:

  • Power Distribution: Understanding power rails, redundancy, and backup battery systems
  • RF Terminations: Proper fiber optic and RF connector termination techniques
  • Strain Relief: Correct use of cable routing, grommets, and protective conduits
  • Torque Specifications: Critical connector torque values to ensure proper connections
  • Environmental Sealing: Weatherproofing techniques to protect equipment in outdoor installations
  • Routine Maintenance: Planned inspection and preventive maintenance schedules

Trainees asked targeted technical questions about equipment operation, environmental considerations, and maintenance best practices. The interactive discussion clarified many practical aspects of SBC deployment.

Trainer explains SBC cabinet architecture and proper cable routing to the field crew

✅ Hands-on Commissioning & Verification

The live commissioning demonstration covered essential verification procedures that trainees performed under direct supervision:

  • Power-up Sequencing: Proper startup procedures and verifying all power supply rails
  • Management Interface Checks: Initial system configuration and reading operational logs
  • RF Verification: Using test equipment to confirm expected signal levels and coverage
  • Basic Troubleshooting: Diagnostic procedures for common issues:
    • Cable continuity and fault detection
    • Ground continuity verification
    • Connector integrity and alignment checks

Each trainee had the opportunity to perform verification steps under expert supervision. This hands-on experience proved invaluable for building confidence and practical skills.

Live commissioning demonstration with trainees observing and performing measurements

📊 Observations & Recommendations

Documentation & Checklists

Recommendation: Produce a concise commissioning checklist with clear pass/fail criteria. This enables faster handovers and ensures consistency across multiple sites. Digital checklists with photographic evidence improve quality control.

Cable Management & Labeling

Recommendation: Use color-coded labeled ties and durable route markers to simplify future maintenance and troubleshooting. Proper documentation with as-built diagrams speeds up issue resolution by 30-40%.

Training Frequency & Continuity

Recommendation: Quarterly refresher training sessions are essential to keep field crews familiar with equipment updates and best practices. Include both new technicians and experienced staff for knowledge sharing.

Safety Culture & Continuous Improvement

Recommendation: Reinforce exclusion zone awareness through regular drills and near-miss reporting. Establish a feedback loop where field technicians can report safety concerns and training needs.
